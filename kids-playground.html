Fostering Well-Rounded Development

At Majestic School, physical play is seen as a central pillar of early education. Our kids playground is designed as a vibrant, safe play space with diverse modules, promoting cardiovascular health, muscular strength, and spatial awareness.

Under the active supervision of trained physical education instructors, our primary and kindergarten students develop social skills like teamwork, cooperation, and conflict resolution during recess.

Soft EPDM Rubber Flooring

Impact-absorbing rubber safety tiles protect children against falls and scraps.

Uncompromised Play Safety Standards

Playtime should be active and joyful, but never risky. We maintain strict playground management processes and safe structural testing standards.

Routine Maintenance Checks

All swing chains, climbing nets, slides, and connection nuts are physically inspected weekly by campus technicians to prevent structural failures.

Dynamic PE Curriculum Integration

Our Outdoor Recess classes are closely mapped to early physical development goals, incorporating motor coordination, minor balance gymnastics, and dynamic speed agility runs.

ROADMAP

Playtime Management Process

1

Supervised Assembly & Warm-Up

Students gather in designated sectors, receive simple instructions, perform basic light stretching, and wear active physical footwear.

2

Active Structured & Free Play

Children engage in swing loops, climbing frameworks, slide tunnels, or minor running athletics under the active eye of 2-3 PE coaches.

3

Cool-Down & Water hydration

Classes end with calm breathing control drills, full hydration, uniform hygiene checks, and orderly return to classrooms.
