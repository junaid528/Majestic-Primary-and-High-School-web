Nurturing Next-Gen Researchers

At Majestic Primary & High School, we believe that science is a verb—it is something you do, not just something you read about. Our unified Science Laboratory is engineered to provide students from Classes 1 to 10 with a safe, fully equipped space to observe, test, and validate scientific laws.

By integrating theory with practical application, our students cultivate critical thinking, scientific reasoning, and collaborative skills that form the foundation of future careers in STEM.

Hands-On Learning Experience

Weekly lab classes mapped dynamically to ICSE and State curricula guidelines.

Rigorous Safety Norms

Fully supervised environments with personal protective equipment and active fire prevention.

Collaborative Learning Space

Empowering group research and experimental validation.

LAB FEATURES

Triple-Discipline Lab Architecture

Our spacious facility contains designated scientific sectors tailored for Physics, Chemistry, and Biology disciplines under one unified space.

Physics Sector

Dedicated optical benches, electrical circuit modules, pendulum arrays, and mechanics instruments that make learning kinematics and electronics intuitive.

  • Ray optics and lens reflection benches
  • Electromagnetic induction setups

Chemistry Sector

Safe reagent storage cupboards, digital balances, fume hoods, titration setups, and specialized burner stations allowing rigorous analytical studies.

  • Acid-Base indicators and titration kit
  • Modern chemical reaction stations

Biology Sector

Compound microscopes, human anatomical models, preserved specimen archives, and slide preparation tables to investigate the micro-world of genetics.

  • High-power digital compound microscopes
  • Extensive plant & animal slide libraries

Uncompromising Safety & Curriculum Mapping

Experimentation must go hand-in-hand with absolute safety. Our laboratory meets strict safety regulations, ensuring peace of mind for parents and a secure learning environment for students.

Smart Safety Integration

Equipped with fire extinguishers, safety goggles, lab aprons, and an emergency eye-wash station. Lab sessions are always supervised by certified science instructors.

Curriculum-Synchronized Experiments

From chemical synthesis reactions in Grade 9 to electromagnetic measurements in Grade 10, every practical class is closely integrated with the corresponding classroom theory lessons.

SAFETY PROTOCOLS

Active Laboratory Safety Countermeasures

Safety is our primary parameter. All students are trained in standard preventive behavior and wear complete shielding equipment.

Safety Goggles

Worn at all times during fluid reactions to prevent volatile eye splatters.

Protective Gloves

Highly resistant nitrile or thermal gloves for secure reagent handling.

White Lab Coats

Protective cotton white lab coats shield clothes and skin from chemicals.

Fire Extinguishers

Easily accessible wall carbon-dioxide suppressors and sandbox buckets.

First Aid Kit

Complete wound care, antiseptics, and burn treatment compounds.

ROADMAP

The Practical Learning Process

1

Pre-Lab Briefing & Safety Checks

Instructors introduce core principles, demonstration protocols, safety criteria, and lab manuals before equipment interaction.

2

Experiential Experimentation

Students work in pairs or small groups of 3-4 to conduct experiments, take rigorous observations, and measure results physically.

3

Report Documentation & Discussion

Practical records are updated, data values evaluated against hypothetical constants, and verified by science professors.
