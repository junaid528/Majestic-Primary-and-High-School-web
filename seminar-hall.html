Nurturing Presentation Confidence

The ability to present ideas clearly on stage is a crucial life skill. At Majestic Primary & High School, our state-of-the-art Seminar Hall acts as a professional platform where students build confidence, refine public speaking skills, and present team research projects.

With fully integrated dynamic acoustics, surround sound, and advanced overhead lighting, this versatile facility hosts a range of academic debates, science exhibitions, and guest lectures.

Premium Acoustic Engineering

Designed with acoustic wall panels and noise isolation to provide clear, echo-free sound.

Dynamic Audio-Visual Control

Fitted with high-definition laser projectors, wide screens, and dynamic wireless microphones.

Academic Presentation Hub

Providing world-class seating and AV integration.

HALL SPECIFICATIONS

Designed with Indigo & Gold Theme

Our seminar layout features cushioned theater seating, wide-angle viewing dimensions, and centralized AV boards.

Ergonomic Theater Chairs

Comfortable, high-back cushioned seating rows configured in an amphitheatrical slope to ensure perfect sightlines from every angle.

  • Wide row-to-row walking spacing

Dynamic Media Board

  • Bluetooth/HDMI podium synchronization
